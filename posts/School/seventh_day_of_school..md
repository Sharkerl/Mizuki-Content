$\cos a \cos b = \dfrac{1}{2}[\cos(\dfrac{a + b}{2}) + \cos(\dfrac{a - b}{2})]$<br>
$\cos^2 x = \dfrac{1}{2}(cos 2x + 1)$<br>
$(\cos^2 x)' = -2\sin x \cos x = -\sin 2x$<br>
$(\sin^2 x)' = 2\sin x \cos x = \sin 2x$<br>
